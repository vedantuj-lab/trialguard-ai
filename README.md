# TrialGuard Insights

MASTER PROMPT — TRIALGUARD AI

PHASE 01 — PRODUCT IDENTITY

Build a production-quality hackathon prototype called:

TrialGuard AI — Governed Clinical Trial Patient Screening & Regulatory Audit Agent

The platform is a clinical-trial screening decision-support system designed to analyze clinical trial protocols and de-identified synthetic patient records, extract structured eligibility criteria, protect PHI, evaluate eligibility through deterministic rules, identify uncertainty and contradictions, and generate evidence-backed audit dossiers.

The product must look like a professional clinical research operations platform rather than a generic chatbot.

Use a modern medical-technology visual language with:

Deep navy

Clinical blue

White

Soft gray

Emerald for successful criteria

Red for failed criteria

Amber for human-review states

Subtle gradients

Glass/soft-card effects where appropriate

Clean typography

Data-dense but readable dashboards

Smooth micro-interactions

Responsive desktop/tablet/mobile layouts

Do not make the UI boring, generic, or excessively futuristic.
